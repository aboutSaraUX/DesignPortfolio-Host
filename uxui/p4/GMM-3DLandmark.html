ABOUTRESUME

Landmark Selection

Introduce 3D maps to users by one click​

Over the next several years, the map will transition to be a more immersive and dimensional experience. We want to introduce a feature, Auto Compute, allows users to tap on a landmark to trigger a tilted 3D view. This action smoothly re-centers the map on the selected landmark with an animated transition. By offering a more detailed and immersive perspective, the 3D view helps users better understand the orientation and layout of a location before visiting, making it especially useful in complex or unfamiliar environments.

Timeline​

3 months design + 1 week sprint (Q3 2023)

Status​

Design delivered to the internal testing tool utilized by over 100k testers.

Team​

PM, Interaction Designer, Motion Designer, UX Researcher, Engineers

My Role​

Interaction Designer

The Scope​

Define and design camera behaviors to introduce smooth tilt 3D experience by selecting landmarks.

Design Statement​

How can the camera behavior be configured, including tilting, rotation, zoom level, and animation, to assist map users in gaining a sense of the area, learning more about a place before visiting, and understanding the orientation?
