EcoEat

"Transform food waste into opportunity with AI."​

EcoEat makes reducing food waste simple, accessible, and impactful. It’s a platform where businesses and individuals connect to sell, donate, or find surplus food with ease. Designed for maximum efficiency, EcoEat empowers users to act sustainably without added complexity. For individuals, EcoEat is a tool for giving or finding food locally. With an AI assistant, users can quickly post surplus food by simply snapping a photo, no tedious forms, no confusion. Gamified features track the weight of food saved and calculate the positive environmental impact, turning sustainability into an engaging experience. Businesses benefit from advanced tools like bulk uploads and AI-driven suggestion, making it easy to manage surplus inventory and connect with bulk buyers or charitable organizations.

The Challenge​

How can we tackle food waste by bridging the gap between information distribution and raising awareness in an engaging and enjoyable way?

The Goal​

Develop B2B and B2C platforms integrating gamification and AI-powered posting to streamline content creation while enhancing user engagement through data visualization.

Design Process 🔍​

Research​

Why food waste matters? 170 millions metric tons CO2 yearly (EPA), food waste = emissions from 42 coal plants, 30% -40% of U.S. food wasted (USDA)

Design Thinking​

Research showed that food waste is driven less by missing solutions and more by low awareness, weak motivation, and difficulty taking action. While existing products like Facebook Marketplace, Too Good To Go, Uber Eats, and Connecteam address parts of the ecosystem, none focus specifically on food waste as a behavior-change problem.

This revealed an opportunity to bridge information distribution and awareness in an engaging, actionable way for users struggling with food waste. EcoEat is designed as a B2B and B2C platform that leverages gamification, AI-powered content creation, and data visualization to lower participation barriers, motivate users, and encourage sustainable behavior change.
