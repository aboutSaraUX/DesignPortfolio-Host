Spotlight

"Spark Your Brain!"​

Spotlight is an interal platform for all employees in company to spread out their efforts during the project process.

Timeline​

2.5 months (Q2 2022)

Platform​

Web Design | Concept Design

Team​

UX/UI Designers(4)

My Role 🧑‍🎨​

Team Lead, User Research, UX/UI Design, User Testing

The Problem​

A female employee needs respect for her work in her career/workplace because her teammates don't realize how much she contributes to their project.

The Scope​

How might we help a female employee to spread out her consistent efforts to her team during the project process?
