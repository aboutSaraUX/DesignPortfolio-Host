Aurora Valley

"The AI Psychotherapist for 8-Minute Somatic Relief."​

Aurora Valley is an AI-driven therapeutic agent that democratizes elite UTEP (Unconscious Trajectory Exploration Process) therapy. Designed for somatic anxiety, it translates clinical-grade expertise into an empathetic companion, Hulu the fox, through a “Soft Shell, Hard Core” approach that delivers rigorous medical logic in warm, conversational interactions. Across three professional healing modes, Aurora Valley assesses emotional and pain states, generates post-session insights, and unlocks narrative rewards. Collectible Hulu cards mirror the user’s inner journey, transforming therapy into an engaging path of self-discovery for the modern stress era.

Timeline​

2 months (Q4 2025)

The Challenge​

How might we translate clinically complex, emotionally sensitive AI therapy into an experience that feels approachable, engaging, and human, without overwhelming users or reducing it to a dry academic tool?

The Goal​

To enhance the therapeutic experience by making it more intuitive, engaging, and emotionally resonant, while preserving its medical credibility and original design intent.

Main Features​

01. Dialogue: Talk with Aura the Fox​

Aura the Fox is a 24/7 AI therapeutic companion that supports emotional release through guided conversation. Users can engage in three modes, free exploration, emotional processing, and dream-based therapy, to safely explore their inner state. By following Aura’s real-time guidance, which combines breathing exercises with cognitive restructuring, users regulate emotions, reframe distress, and experience immediate relief, making therapy feel as natural as a conversation.

02. Gamification: Collect Cards​

Users collect healing fragments from Aura the Fox’s stories to unlock life stories cards. Each card reflects emotional insights and personal growth, turning therapeutic moments into meaningful rewards. The sense of progression and collection encourages continued engagement over time, helping users build emotional awareness and resilience through a gentle, story-driven experience.

03. Record & Assessment​

Aurora Valley integrates clinically validated scales including PHQ-9 and GAD-7 alongside a pentagon stress index to visualize emotional change over time. These records transform abstract feelings into clear, trackable insights. By grounding each session in quantitative assessment, the feature provides scientific evidence of therapeutic progress while helping users better understand and reflect on their emotional state.

Design Process 🔍​

Overview​

The client provided an initial design draft and asked our team to improve key parts of the product, including the conversational flow, in-app gamification, and the post-session summary report. Working with an existing product introduced real-world constraints: we needed to respect the original concept, improve usability, and enhance user engagement without redesigning the app from scratch.
