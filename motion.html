Today is a Day! Motion Graphic

One year daily project | 2018 | After Effects | Cinema4D | Illustrator

Brief: It is the same every day, but different. Finding the meaning for each day and making it special are very important for you. This project informs the audience about special events happening every day through a daily update.

Zodiac Signs and Chinese Zodiac

3 months | 2018 | After Effects

Brief: The order of the Zodiac Signs is Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces. The Chinese Zodiac, known as Sheng Xiao, is based on a twelve-year cycle, each year in that cycle related to an animal sign. These signs are the rat, ox, tiger, rabbit, dragon, snake, horse, sheep, monkey, rooster, dog and pig.

Fisherman, 3D Short Animation

3 months | 2017 | Maya | After Effects

Brief: This is a personal 3D short animation which was designed in 2017.  The story is about a wheelchair-bound old man who goes fishing. The time passes, but the old man only catches one fish. When sunset arrives, a magical thing happens after he gets up and jumps into the lake.
